If you believe this is an emergency, seek immediate local emergency medical help.

Ayusetu is a case-preparation tool, not an emergency response service. It cannot see, dispatch, or contact emergency services on your behalf. If you or someone nearby is in danger, act now using the guidance below.

Call local emergency number ← Back to Ayusetu
01

Call your local emergency number immediately, or have someone nearby call for you.

02

If you're with someone who is unconscious, not breathing normally, or bleeding severely, do not wait to look up information — get emergency help on the way first.

03

Stay on the line with emergency services and follow their instructions exactly.

04

If safe to do so, unlock doors, gather medications and ID, and wait somewhere visible for responders to arrive.

05

Once the person is safe and stable, you can use Ayusetu to prepare a case for their follow-up care.
